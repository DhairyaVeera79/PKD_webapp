His Life

The Great Spiritual Luminary

Over 2,500 years ago, Lord Mahavir, the 24th Jain Tirthankara, attained nirvana. Since then, many Enlightened Souls have carried forward His legacy. Over 150 years ago, Shrimad Rajchandraji scaled great spiritual heights and demystified the path to self-discovery.

🌟

Extraordinary Childhood

Born in Vavaniya (1867), He recollected past lives at age 7 (Jatismaranjnan). By age 8, He had written ~5,000 couplets and delivered speeches on diverse subjects.

🧠

Avdhan Mastery

Demonstrated Shatavdhan (100 simultaneous tasks) at age 19 in Mumbai, astonishing scholars. Awarded gold medal and titles “Sakshat Saraswati” and “Hind na Heera.”

Avdhan events →
✨

Self-Realisation

Attained Shuddh Samyagdarshan at ~age 23. Deliberately ceased avdhans, calling them “tinsel compared to gold.” Guided devotees to the path of liberation.

📖

Profound Compositions

Authored Mokshmala (108 lessons) at 16, Bhavnabodh at 18, and the crown jewel — Shri Atmasiddhi Shastra (142 verses) in a single sitting at age 28.

🛐

Intense Retreats

From 1896 onwards, retreated to Kavitha, Ralaj, Vadva, Uttarsanda, Kheda, and Idar — practising extreme austerities and guiding monks to self-realisation.

Eternal Legacy

Left His mortal body at age 33 in Rajkot (1901) in a state of absolute samadhi. Founded the Paramshrut Prabhavak Mandal. His teachings continue to transform lives.

Sacred Writings

Literary Works

Simple, precise, and yet immensely profound — Param Krupalu Dev’s literary works are an expression of His supreme inner state. Laying down a clear and complete path to spiritual living, one read is enough to answer existential questions, while every next read takes you deeper, until neither the questioner nor question remains.

From the vast archives of His philosophical literature, the crown jewel is Shri Atmasiddhi Shastra, a 142-verse poetic masterpiece composed in a single sitting at dusk in Nadiad on Aso Vad 1, V.S. 1952 (October 29, 1896).

A Historic Bond

Param Krupalu Dev and Gandhiji

Mahatma Gandhi met Shrimad Rajchandraji in Mumbai in 1891, having just returned from England as a young barrister. This meeting profoundly shaped Gandhiji’s spiritual worldview. He continued to seek Param Krupalu Dev’s guidance through letters, posing his now-famous 27 questions on religion and ethics.

“Such was the man who captivated my heart in religious matters as no other man has till now.”

— Mahatma Gandhi, Modern Review, June 1930

The Living Legacy

Pujya Gurudevshri Rakeshji

Pujya Gurudevshri Rakeshji, the founder of Shrimad Rajchandra Mission Dharampur (SRMD), is an Enlightened Master who has dedicated His life to propagating the teachings of Param Krupalu Dev. Through satsangs, meditation retreats, and the establishment of the Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram in Dharampur, He has made Param Krupalu Dev’s wisdom accessible to millions of seekers worldwide.

“If I am asked to give a brief introduction of Param Krupalu Dev, I would say that Shrimad Rajchandra means an invitation to turn within. This invitation card has just two words — Shrimad Rajchandra. But the moment it is received, one feels inspired to connect with the self.”

— Pujya Gurudevshri Rakeshji

“Shrimad Rajchandraji is not just a historic event, it is an existence absorbed in the stream of eternal consciousness. Shrimad Rajchandraji is not this outer frame, it is consciousness revelling in the state beyond the body.”

— Pujya Gurudevshri Rakeshji
