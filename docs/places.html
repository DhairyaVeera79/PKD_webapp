Places of Major Significance

In roughly chronological order of Param Krupalu Dev's association with each place.

  • Vavania (Vavania-mota) — Birthplace and childhood home. Morbi district, Saurashtra. Ravjibhai's family settled here from V.S. 1892. Param Krupalu Dev born Kartik Purnima V.S. 1924. Jatismaranjnan at age 7.
  • Morbi — Schooling (V.S. 1937–1940). Avdhan experiments began here. Place of marriage (V.S. 1944). Anglo-Vernacular school; Darbar Saheb's office.
  • Jamnagar — Regional avdhan demonstrations.
  • Wadhwan Camp (Surendranagar) — 16 avdhans performed (Colonel H. L. Natsaheb present). Famous photographs of Param Krupalu Dev taken here during a state of extreme dispassion.
  • Limbdi — Saubhagyabhai's father Lallubhai was state administrator here before settling in Sayla.
  • Botad — 52 avdhans performed in presence of Sheth Harilal Shivlal. Manilal Gandhi's residence.
  • Jetpar — Home of Chatrabhuj Bechar (Shivkunvarben's husband). First meeting of Saubhagyabhai with Param Krupalu Dev (Bhadarva V.S. 1946). Param Krupalu Dev frequently stayed here.
  • Ahmedabad — Juthabhai's home (Cheepa Pol). Sheth Dalpatbhai Bhagubhai's compound (52 avdhans). Ambalalbhai attained self-realisation here (V.S. 1957). Mokshmala published (V.S. 1944).
  • Mumbai (Bombay) — Where Param Krupalu Dev lived and conducted business from ~V.S. 1945. Shatavdhan at Framjee Cawasjee Institute (Jan 22, 1887). Met Gandhiji (V.S. 1947). Revashankar Jagjivan Co. headquarters.
  • Khambhat (Cambay) — Ambalalbhai's native place. Lalluji Muni initiated as monk (V.S. 1940) and first met Param Krupalu Dev here (Dhanteras V.S. 1946). Ambalalbhai's death (V.S. 1963).
  • Kavitha (Kawitha) — Near Nadiad. Param Krupalu Dev's first major spiritual retreat (V.S. 1952). Spent Paryushan here. Nightly meditations by Vagadia lake. Incident with bandit Babardeva.
  • Uttarsanda (Untarsanda) — Charotar region, near Anand. Param Krupalu Dev's intense 2nd retreat (V.S. 1953). Elevated spiritual state manifesting extraordinary radiance. Motilal Bhavsar's bungalow.
  • Vaso — Lalluji Muni spent monsoons here. Param Krupalu Dev imparted wisdom for one hour on His last day (V.S. 1954) — moment of Lalluji Muni's self-realisation.
  • Idar — North Gujarat. Param Krupalu Dev's final spiritual retreat (V.S. 1955/1956). Dr. Pranjivandas was Chief Medical Officer of Idar State. Exploration of caves; intense meditation.
  • Rajkot — Site of Param Krupalu Dev's passing — Chaitra Vad 5, V.S. 1957 (April 9, 1901) at 7:17 PM. Memorial (samadhi sthal) built here.

Centres of Devotee Activity

  • Sayla (Shayla) — Saurashtra. 'The village of the devotees'. Home of Saubhagyabhai Lallubhai and Dungarshibhai Gosalia. Also Jijiben's marital home.
  • Agas — Near Anand. Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram (est. Jeth Sud 5, V.S. 1976). Founded by Lalluji Muni on 7.5 acres donated by Jijibhai Kuberdas of Sandesar. Lalluji Muni passed away here V.S. 1992.
  • Dharampur — South Gujarat (near Valsad). Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram (est. 2001). Founded by Pujya Gurudevshri Rakeshji. 223 acres on hillock of Mohangadh. Temple, meditation halls, hospital.

Other Places in Gujarat & Saurashtra

  • Surat — Home of Naginchand Kapurchand (jeweller), associate of Revashankar Jagjivan Co.
  • Bharuch (Broach) — Home of Anupchand Malukchandbhai. Param Krupalu Dev studied 'Bhadrabahusanhita' here.
  • Vadodara (Baroda) — Home of Maneklal Ghelabhai and his father, an expert gemstone valuator.
  • Nadiad — Charotar region. Near Kavitha; part of the area where Param Krupalu Dev's retreats occurred.
  • Anand — Near Uttarsanda. Part of Charotar region where retreats occurred.
  • Kheda — Devkaranji Muni saw Param Krupalu Dev here; struck by His deeply emaciated yet radiant state. Poojabhai Someshwar Bhatt visited here.
  • Ralaj — Near Jetpar. Connected to devotees' movement.
  • Vadva — Near Bhavnagar. Connected to Param Krupalu Dev's travels.
  • Viramgam — Hometown of Sukhlal Chaganlal.
  • Naroda — Near Ahmedabad. Connected to Param Krupalu Dev's activities.
  • Hadamtala — Where Manilal Gandhi met Param Krupalu Dev.
  • Ranpur — Connected to Param Krupalu Dev's travels in Saurashtra.
  • Tithal — Near Valsad. Connected to the mission's activities.
  • Vataman — In Bhal region. Birthplace of Lalluji Muni.
  • Sandesar — Home of Jijibhai Kuberdas, who donated 7.5 acres for Agas Ashram.
  • Fenav — Hometown of Chhotalal Kapurchand.
  • Chamanpar — Near Vavania. Where Ravjibhai conducted moneylending business.
  • Maliya — Maternal grandfather Raghavjibhai's home.
  • Manekwada — Original home of Panchanbhai before moving to Vavania (V.S. 1892).
  • Sihol — 12 miles from Kavitha. Home of Shamalbhai's daughter Heera.
  • Anjar (Kutch) — Meenaben's marital home (married Tokarshi Pitamber).

Places Outside Gujarat & International

  • Pune — Param Krupalu Dev visited for business. Nanchandbhai's location.
  • Chennai — Connected to Param Krupalu Dev's travels.
  • Chicago — Parliament of World's Religions (September 1893). Virchand Raghavji Gandhi cited Param Krupalu Dev's capabilities.
  • Phoenix, Durban (South Africa) — Gandhiji's Phoenix Ashram (V.S. 1960). Gandhiji's celibacy vow taken here (V.S. 1962).
  • London — Gandhiji's bundle of Param Krupalu Dev's letters accidentally left in a bus here.
  • Yerwada Jail, Pune — Gandhiji began writing memoirs about Param Krupalu Dev here.
