Chitrapats (also known as Morvi) is a city and former princely state in the Gujarat state of India. It is situated on the Machhu River, 35 km from the sea and 60 km from Rajkot. Juthabhai is significant in the life of Shrimad Rajchandraji, a revered spiritual figure, and is known for its historical and spiritual importance.
