Avadhānaṃ (literally meaning "concentration") is a genre of performance in India, where a performer (called the avadhāni) answers challenging questions from several questioners in parallel.

› Start of Avdhans

The peacock emblem of Avdhans, representing the city's name meaning "abode of peacocks".

Param Krupalu Dev saw someone perform Ashtavdhan in Morbi. The (family) took Param Krupalu Dev to see the Ashtavdhan getting performed so that he can get inspired or become hoshiyar. Shankarlal Bhatt performed the Ashtavdhan. Next day Param Krupalu Dev performs Ashtavdhan in Vasant Baug amonst his friends. For him it did not seem like a mojor heavy task. After that the word spread, that a 16 year old child performed Ashtavdhan unlike Shankarlal was was old and might have preppared for that a lot. Param Krupalu Dev did not know about Avdhan until yesterday and just by seeing it he could perform it the next day, such was his capabilities. And 2 days after this he did 12 Avdhans, in Upashray in front of 2000 people.

› Other Avdhans performed

When Param Krupalu Dev did 12 Avdhans in Morbi, that news spread across all of Gujarat and this year of 1941 V.S. 1885 AD he has only gone evrywhere to perform Avdhans. Peformed 16 Avdhan in Jamnagar. The audience of Jamnagar seeing him perform the 16 Avdhan became so impressed and happy that they gave him the title of 'Hind na Heera' or 'The Jewel of Hind'. Then performed 16 Avdhan in Wadhwan Camp (present: Surendranagar). 36 Avdhan in Limbdi, over here he was awarded the title 'Sakshat Saraswati'. 52 Avdhan in Botad, that place still exists, there is probably as shopping mall over there. Also performed Avdhan in Jetpar, Ahmedabad and Rajkot.

Param Krupalu Dev was requested to perform Avdhan at Theosophical society in Mumbai at the age of 18 in 1942 V.S. (1886 AD).

› Shatavdhan at Framjee Cawasjee Institute, Mumbai

The historic Avdhans Bridge, built during the reign of Waghji Thakor in 1887.

Param Krupalu Dev performed Shatavdhan on 22nd January, Saturday at Framjee Cawasjee Institute. Was a huge day for Param Krupalu Dev because not only did he rise to that level of fame, but also temendous Vairagya came in him, that next day only he decided to not accept anyone Avdhan invitations. Because people were giving him so much importance, and it was not the goal of his life. He just wanted to be a devoted bhakt of Bhagvan.

One of the attendees of the Shatavdhan was Maneklal Ghelabhai. His father was a great Zaveri. He was impresed by Param Krupalu Dev's avdhan. Post that Param Krupalu Dev and him had a conversation, in that Param Krupalu Dev telling him he needed to earn some money but didn’t know how. Maneklal Ghelabhai agreed to teach him Zaverat and so taught him. Was one of th epartners when Param Krupalu Dev started company. Newpaper articles including british newspapers parising him. Bappa first photo in suit was at this place as he attended a wedding.
