Events in chronological order

› Pre-Birth

  • Grandfather Shri Panchanbhai moves from Manekwada (Morbi district) to Vavania (V.S. 1892 / 1836 AD)
  • Father Shri Ravjibhai born (V.S. 1902 / ~1846 AD)
  • Ravjibhai begins moneylending business at age 14 in Vavania
  • Ravjibhai marries Devbai, daughter of Shri Raghavjibhai of Maliya
  • Yogini Rambaiba of Vavania predicts Devbai will bear an extraordinary son
  • A fakir served by Ravjibhai predicts he will have an illustrious son
  • Peace treaty between Kutch and Morbi over Vavania's bay waters; harbour restored, trade flourished (V.S. 1923 / Maha-Fagan, while Param Krupalu Dev was in mother's womb)

› V.S. 1924 | 1867 AD | Birth

  • Birth of Shrimad Rajchandra (named Laxminandan) on Kartik Purnima (Devdiwali), Sunday, November 10, 1867, at 2:00 AM in Vavania, Saurashtra

› V.S. 1928 | ~1872 AD | Age 4

  • Name changed from Laxminandan to Raichand

› V.S. 1931 | 1875 AD | Age 7

  • Jatismaranjnan (recollection of past lives) triggered by witnessing cremation of Shri Amichandbhai (died of snakebite) in Vavania
  • Started formal education at school; teacher Shri Lavjibhai astonished by His instant mastery

› V.S. ~1931–1933 | ~1875–1877 AD | Age 7–9

  • Completed Gujarati curriculum equivalent to 7 standards in just 2 years

› V.S. ~1932 | ~1876 AD | Age 8

  • Written ~5,000 couplets (not currently available)

› V.S. ~1933 | ~1877 AD | Age 9

  • Studied Ramayana and Mahabharata and wove them into verse

› V.S. ~1934 | ~1878 AD | Age 10

  • Could deliver interesting speeches on many subjects
  • Family undergoing severe financial crisis; Devbai had to sell jewellery

› V.S. ~1935 | ~1879 AD | Age 11

  • Wrote mature articles published in 'Buddhiprakash' magazine; won prizes for essays

› V.S. ~1936 | ~1880 AD | Age 12

  • Wrote 300 couplets on a clock in 3 days

› V.S. ~1937 | ~1881 AD | Age 13

  • Left for Rajkot to study English
  • Became fully coloured by Jainism; kanthi (Vaishnav beaded necklace from Shri Ramdasji) broke and was not worn again
  • Began intense study of scriptures related to detachment and truth
  • Read all the Agams (Jain canons) in 15 months
  • Joined father's moneylending business in Vavania after discontinuing studies

› V.S. 1937–1940 | 1881–1884 AD | Age 13–16

  • Period of 'Religious Churning' — deliberation on six philosophies (Vedanta, Sankhya, Nyaya, Buddhism, Jainism, etc.)

› V.S. 1940 | 1884 AD | Age 16

  • Authored 'Mokshmala' (108 lessons) at age 16 years 5 months, completed in just 3 days
  • First 8 Avdhans performed after watching Shastri Shankarlal Maheshwar Bhatt in Morbi; repeated next day
  • Performed 12 Avdhans in Morbi's sacred Jain shelter to an audience of 2,000
  • Performed 12 and 16 Avdhans in Jamnagar; honoured with title 'The Jewel of Hind'
  • Performed 16 Avdhans in Wadhwan before 2,000 people; covered by Gujarati, Mumbai Samachar, Lokmitra, Nyayadarshak
  • Performed 52 Avdhans in Botad in presence of Sheth Shri Harilal Shivlal
  • Demonstrated Avdhan powers in Jetpar, Ahmedabad and other places
  • Invited to Kutch by diwan Shri Manibhai Jashbhai; delivered captivating lecture on religion
  • First Chitrapat (photograph)

› V.S. 1941 | 1885 AD | Age 17

  • Avdhans continue throughout the region

› V.S. 1942 | 1886 AD | Age 18

  • Authored 'Bhavnabodh' (12 bhavnas with examples and illustrations)
  • First visited Mumbai (Bhadarva V.S. 1942) with Avdhan programme and financial prospects
  • Stopped in Jetpar at brother-in-law Shri Chatrabhuj Bechar's home (Aso V.S. 1942); Astrologer Shri Shankar Pancholi made a horoscope for Him

› V.S. 1943 | 1887 AD | Age 19

  • Performed Avdhans extensively in Mumbai
  • Performed Avdhans at Theosophical Society in Mumbai (9 languages)
  • Shatavdhan (100 Avdhans) at Framjee Cawasjee Institute, Mumbai, January 22, 1887 (Saturday); presided by Dr. Peterson; awarded gold medal and title 'Sakshat Saraswati'
  • Demonstrated 'nashtavidya' of astrology in Jetpar (Aso V.S. 1943); Shri Shankar Pancholi was spellbound
  • Came to Mumbai to search for business
  • Met Shri Maneklal Ghelabhai of Vadodara at Shatavdhan; learnt jewellery valuation from him

› V.S. 1944 | 1888 AD | Age 20

  • Departed Mumbai for Vavania for marriage (Posh V.S. 1944)
  • Married Jhabakbai (daughter of Shri Popatlalbhai Mehta) in Morbi (Maha Sud 12, V.S. 1944)
  • While returning from wedding in Morbi, saffron water drizzled on His vehicle — bestowed title 'Yugpradhan'
  • First meeting with Shri Juthabhai during publication of Mokshmala in Ahmedabad
  • Stayed 2–2.5 months in Ahmedabad for publishing Mokshmala; letter of recommendation from Shri Vinaychand Popatbhai Daftari
  • Avdhan demonstration in Shri Dalpatbhai Bhagubhai's compound in Ahmedabad
  • Total stop of public Avdhans

› V.S. 1945 | 1889 AD | Age 21

  • Shri Revashankarbhai Jagjivan moved from Morbi to Mumbai (Ashadh V.S. 1945)
  • Param Krupalu Dev joined Shri Revashankarbhai as partner in jewellery business (Bhadarva V.S. 1945)
  • Wrote 'My Views on Women'
  • Shri Ambalalbhai and Shri Chhotalalbhai met Shri Juthabhai at a wedding in Ahmedabad; Ambalalbhai inspired (Vaishakh V.S. 1945)
  • Param Krupalu Dev visited Ahmedabad, stayed at Shri Juthabhai's residence in Cheepa Pol for 15 days (Kartik V.S. 1945)
  • Stayed in Bharuch at home of Sheth Shri Anupchand Malukchandbhai; studied 'Shri Bhadrabahusanhita'

› V.S. 1946 | 1890 AD | Age 22

  • Revashankar Jagjivan Co. established (Fagan Vad 11, V.S. 1946) — Param Krupalu Dev, Revashankarbhai, Maneklalbhai as partners
  • First son Chaganbhai born
  • First meeting with Shri Saubhagyabhai at Jetpar, at Shri Chatrabhuj Bechar's home (1st Bhadarva V.S. 1946)
  • Param Krupalu Dev visited Khambhat at Shri Ambalalbhai's request; first meeting with Shri Lalluji Muni at Sthanakvasi Upashray (Aso Vad 12, V.S. 1946)
  • Shri Juthabhai passed away at age 23 (Ashadh Sud 9, V.S. 1946)

› V.S. 1947 | 1891 AD | Age 23

  • Attained Shuddh Samyagdarshan (self-realisation)
  • Wrote to Saubhagyabhai confirming self-realisation: "The knot has been severed" (Kartik Sud 14, V.S. 1947)
  • Spent Paryushan in village of Ralaj, near Khambhat; composed 4 poems
  • First meeting with Mahatma Gandhi at home of Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta in Mumbai (Jeth V.S. 1947); Gandhiji had just returned from England as a barrister

› V.S. 1948 | 1892 AD | Age 24

  • Shri Naginchand Kapurchand (Surat) and Shri Chhotalal Lallubhai (Ahmedabad) joined the firm
  • First daughter Javalben born
  • Business obligations intensified significantly

› V.S. 1948–1951 | 1892–1895 AD | Age 24–27

  • Period of virtual silence — curtailed spiritual writing and discussions due to heavy business obligations

› V.S. 1949 | 1893 AD | Age 25

  • Gandhiji left for Africa (summer V.S. 1949)
  • Lalluji Muni spent 4 monsoon months at upashray in Chinchpokli, Mumbai; visited Param Krupalu Dev daily

› V.S. 1950 | 1894 AD | Age 26

  • Second daughter Kashiben born
  • Lalluji Muni spent monsoon months in Surat; requested self-realisation; Param Krupalu Dev sent 'Chha Padno Patra' (Patrank-493)

› V.S. 1951 | 1895 AD | Age 27

  • Commenced preparations to retire from business

› V.S. 1952 | 1896 AD | Age 28

  • Younger brother Shri Mansukhbhai joined the firm
  • Second son Ratilalbhai born
  • Expressed desire to retire from business; stayed on as advisor only (Jeth V.S. 1952)
  • Left Mumbai for Charotar district — beginning of intensive retreats (Shravan V.S. 1952)
  • Retreat in Kavitha — stayed at Shri Jhaverchand Sheth's residence; meditated at Mithuji's well, Bhaidva well, Vajigorani's pastureland
  • Retreat in Ralaj — stayed at Parsi's bungalow; spent Paryushan there
  • Retreat in Vadva — 6 days with Lalluji Muni and other monks
  • In Khambhat — at home of Shri Chhotalal Manekchand
  • Composed 'Mool Marg Sambhlo Jinno Re' in Anand (Aso Sud Ekam, V.S. 1952)
  • Composed 'Shri Atmasiddhi Shastra' (142 verses) in a single sitting (1.5–2 hours) at dusk in Nadiad, day after Sharad Purnima (Aso Vad 1, V.S. 1952); Shri Ambalalbhai held lantern

› V.S. 1952–1953 | 1896–1897 AD | Age 28–29

  • Stayed in Nadiad after Diwali
  • Went to Vavania for mother's illness
  • Composed 'Apurva Avsar' in Vavania

› V.S. 1953 | 1897 AD | Age 29

  • Travelled with Saubhagyabhai to Idar for 10 days; King of Idar visited Param Krupalu Dev (Vaishakh V.S. 1953)
  • Shri Saubhagyabhai passed away at 10:50 AM, Thursday (Jeth Vad 10, V.S. 1953); Param Krupalu Dev intuitively took cold water bath at the same moment
  • Returned to Mumbai from Idar (Jeth V.S. 1953)

› V.S. 1954 | 1898 AD | Age 30

  • Resided in Morbi for 3 months (Maha–Chaitra V.S. 1954); sermons published as 'Vyakhyansaar-1'
  • Retreat in Kavitha — discourses on Shri Thanang Sutra, Yoga Vasishtha, Vicharsagar (Shravan V.S. 1954)
  • Retreat in Vaso — spent 1 month with monks; Lalluji Muni attained self-realisation on last day of retreat (Bhadarva V.S. 1954)
  • Retreat in Uttarsanda — stayed at small bungalow near a pond, 2 miles from Nadiad; extreme austerities with Shri Motilal Bhavsar
  • Retreat in Kheda — resided in bungalow outside the village for 23 days

› V.S. 1955 | 1899 AD | Age 31

  • Returned briefly to Mumbai
  • Idar retreat — stayed at Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta's residence; meditated in hills, caves, Chandan caves; spent 6 weeks in an ancient cave (from Magsar Sud 5, V.S. 1955)
  • Monks visited Him in Idar for 5 days; taught 'Bruhad Dravyasangrah' seated on 'Pudhvishila' and 'Siddhashila'
  • Proclaimed renunciation of wealth, wife, son etc. at a gathering
  • In Naroda, walked barefoot in scorching heat; blessed monks with darshan
  • Completely retired from business; willed entire assets to brother Mansukhbhai
  • Told Ambalalbhai about his indolence; predicted his future self-realisation

› V.S. 1956 | 1900 AD | Age 32

  • Body struck by incurable disease — first appeared as weakness, diagnosed as colitis (Posh V.S. 1956)
  • Retreated to Dharampur for change of weather; treated by Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta (Chaitra V.S. 1956)
  • Visited Ahmedabad, Viramgam, Vavania (~2 months), Morbi
  • Left Morbi for Wadhwan Camp; stayed at Limbdi State guest house (Shravan Vad 10, V.S. 1956)
  • Founded 'Paramshrut Prabhavak Mandal' in Wadhwan Camp
  • Dictated 108 lessons of 'Pragnavbodh Mokshamala'
  • Two photographs taken at Shri Sukhlalbhai's request — lotus posture and standing meditative pose

› V.S. 1957 | 1900–1901 AD | Age 33

  • Left Wadhwan Camp for Ahmedabad; stayed 27 days at Aga Khan's bungalow on Sabarmati River (Kartak Vad 7)
  • Wrote 12 vows for mother Devba; sent her and Jhabakba to take vows from monks
  • Shri Ambalalbhai attained self-realisation in Ahmedabad through Param Krupalu Dev's "unprecedented showers of wisdom"
  • Last meeting with Lalluji Muni and Devkaranji Muni: "Do not differentiate between me and the Dispassionate Lord"
  • Arrived in Mumbai from Ahmedabad; health deteriorating (Magsar Vad)
  • Taken to Matunga, Sion (Mumbai), Tithal (near Valsad) for recovery
  • Returned to Wadhwan Camp; body reduced from 130–140 lbs to 57 lbs (Maha Vad 6)
  • Left Wadhwan Camp; arrived at Rajkot where He stayed until the end (Fagan Sud 6)
  • Composed final poem — last message (Chaitra Sud 9)
  • Last words to Mansukhbhai, Revashankarbhai, Narbherambhai: "Do not worry, this Soul is eternal" (Chaitra Vad 4, evening — April 8, 1901)
  • Caught severe cold at 2:30 AM; said "Your brother's death will be in the state of samadhi" (Chaitra Vad 5 — April 9, 1901)
  • Instructed transfer to reclining couch for meditative state (7:30 AM)
  • Final words: "Mansukh, do not be sad. Take care of mother. I am now abiding in the Self." (8:45 AM)
  • Dehvilay — Param Krupalu Dev left His mortal body in Rajkot in a state of absolute samadhi (Chaitra Vad 5, 2:00 PM, Tuesday — April 9, 1901)

› Post-Death Events

  • Passing covered by Mumbai Samachar, The Pioneer, The Indian Spectator and other dailies (V.S. 1957 / 1901)
  • Gandhiji wrote to Revashankarbhai from Durban expressing grief (May 21, 1901)
  • First edition of 'Shrimad Rajchandra' (collected works) published by Ambalalbhai and Mansukhbhai (V.S. 1961 / 1905)
  • Shri Ambalalbhai passed away at age 37, uttering 'Sahajatmaswaroop Hey Prabhu' (Chaitra Vad 12, V.S. 1963 / ~1907)
  • Shri Chaganbhai (Param Krupalu Dev's first son) died of tuberculosis at age 19 (V.S. 1965 / ~1909)
  • Param Krupalu Dev's wife Jhabakbai passed away (V.S. 1969 / ~1913)
  • Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram, Agas established on farmland donated by Shri Jijibhai Kuberdas of Sandesar (Jeth Sud 5, V.S. 1976 / ~1920)
  • Second edition of 'Shrimad Rajchandra' published with Gandhiji's preface (V.S. 1982 / ~1926)
  • Shri Lalluji Muni passed away in Agas Ashram in unprecedented samadhi at 8:10 PM (Vaishakh Sud 8, V.S. 1992 / ~1936)
  • Javalben (Param Krupalu Dev's first daughter) passed away (V.S. 2034 / ~1978)
