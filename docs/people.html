Bhakt Ratnas (Key Devotees)

  • Pujyashree Juthabhai Ujamshibhai — First devotee. 'Satyaparayan'. Met Param Krupalu Dev V.S. 1944 in Ahmedabad. Passed away V.S. 1946, age 23.
  • Pujyashree Saubhagyabhai Lallubhai — 'Hridayroop'. Divine soulmate. Born V.S. 1880, Sayla. Passed away V.S. 1953 in samadhi.
  • Pujyashree Ambalalbhai Lalchand — Of Khambhat. Born V.S. 1926. Attained self-realisation V.S. 1957. Passed away V.S. 1963, age 37.
  • Pujyashree Lalluji Muni (Shri Laghuraj Swami) — 'Muni of the fourth era'. Born V.S. 1910, Vataman. Established Agas Ashram. Passed away V.S. 1992.

Mahatma Gandhiji

  • Mahatma Gandhi (Mohandas) — ~1.75 years younger than Param Krupalu Dev. Met V.S. 1947 at Dr. Pranjivandas's home. Regarded Param Krupalu Dev as foremost of his three spiritual guides (Param Krupalu Dev, Ruskin, Tolstoy). Frequented Param Krupalu Dev's office daily for 2 years.
