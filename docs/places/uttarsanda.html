Uttarsanda

Uttarsanda, Charotar region, Gujarat
Uttarsanda (Untarsanda)
Region Charotar, Gujarat (near Anand / Nadiad)
Significance Site of intense spiritual retreat; Param Krupalu Dev manifested extraordinary radiance
Period V.S. 1954 (1898 AD)
Key locations Small bungalow near a pond (2 miles from Nadiad); open grounds; pipal tree
Key events Deep meditation retreat with extreme austerities; spiritual dialogues; Navalben told of her liberation

Uttarsanda (also spelt Untarsanda) is a village in Gujarat's Charotar region, about two miles from Nadiad and near Anand. In V.S. 1954 (1898 AD), Shrimad Rajchandra undertook an intense spiritual retreat here — His second consecutive Paryushan spent in seclusion. He stayed at a small bungalow near a pond, found for Him by Shri Motilal Bhavsar. During this retreat, Param Krupalu Dev was in a profoundly elevated spiritual state, manifesting an extraordinary radiance that left a deep impression on all who witnessed it. The Uttarsanda retreat, along with those at Kavitha and Vaso in the same period, represents the high point of Param Krupalu Dev's years of intensive spiritual practice in the Charotar countryside.

› The retreat

In V.S. 1954 (1898 AD), following His second retreat in Kavitha and a month-long stay with monks in Vaso, Param Krupalu Dev continued His retreats in the Charotar region. Shri Motilal Bhavsar, a devoted follower, found a small bungalow near a pond, approximately two miles from Nadiad, in the village of Uttarsanda. Param Krupalu Dev settled here for a period of intense meditation and austerity.

The retreat was marked by extreme austerities. Param Krupalu Dev practised deep meditation in the open grounds and beneath a pipal tree. Shri Motilal Bhavsar and Shri Laherabhai attended to Him during this period. The isolation and simplicity of the setting — a small bungalow in open countryside near a pond — suited the profound inward withdrawal that characterised Param Krupalu Dev's spiritual practice during these years.

› Extraordinary radiance

Those present during the Uttarsanda retreat witnessed Param Krupalu Dev in an elevated spiritual state manifesting extraordinary radiance. According to accounts, His body, face, and demeanour radiated a luminosity that was visible to those around Him — a phenomenon that went beyond ordinary physical appearance and was directly connected to the depth of His inner realisation.

Param Krupalu Dev engaged in spiritual dialogues with those present, and His ecstatic states during meditation made a lasting impression. This period is considered one of the peaks of Param Krupalu Dev's outward manifestation of His inner spiritual attainment, before the onset of illness that would mark His final years.

› Navalben's blessing

A notable event during the Uttarsanda retreat was Param Krupalu Dev's interaction with Navalben, the wife of Shri Motilal Bhavsar. Param Krupalu Dev told Navalben that she would attain liberation in her 8th life — a remarkably specific spiritual prophecy that spoke to both her accumulated merit and Param Krupalu Dev's extraordinary perception of the soul's journey across lifetimes.

Such declarations are extremely rare in the Jain tradition, where knowledge of another being's spiritual trajectory is attributed only to the most advanced souls. This pronouncement has been preserved in the oral tradition of Param Krupalu Dev's followers as evidence of the extraordinary level of His spiritual vision.
