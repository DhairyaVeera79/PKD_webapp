Jamnagar is a city in Saurashtra, Gujarat. In approximately V.S. 1940 (1884 AD), Shrimad Rajchandra performed 12 and 16 avdhans (simultaneous attention demonstrations) in Jamnagar. The audience was so deeply impressed by the young Param Krupalu Dev's extraordinary powers that they bestowed upon Him the title 'Hind na Heera' — The Jewel of Hind — one of the earliest public honours He received. This was part of a broader tour of avdhan demonstrations across Saurashtra, including performances in Morbi, Wadhwan, Botad, Jetpar, and Ahmedabad.

Main article: Avdhans

At approximately age 16, Param Krupalu Dev demonstrated His remarkable powers of simultaneous attention (avdhan) across several cities in Saurashtra. In Jamnagar, He performed both 12 and 16 avdhans — maintaining multiple mental tasks at once with perfect accuracy. The impressed citizens of Jamnagar honoured Him with the title 'Hind na Heera' (The Jewel of Hind), recognising His prodigious abilities as unparalleled in the land.

These demonstrations in Jamnagar were among the early performances that built Param Krupalu Dev's extraordinary reputation, which would eventually lead to the famous Shatavdhan (100 avdhans) at the Framjee Cawasjee Institute in Mumbai in V.S. 1943 (1887 AD).
