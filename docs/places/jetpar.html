Region Saurashtra, Gujarat, India
Significance Home of Chatrabhuj Bechar (Shivkunvarben's husband); first meeting of Saubhagyabhai with Param Krupalu Dev
Period V.S. ~1940–1946 (1884–1890 AD)
Key events First meeting of Saubhagyabhai and Param Krupalu Dev (Bhadarva V.S. 1946); avdhan demonstrations; astrologer Shankar Pancholi's horoscope

Jetpar is a town near Morbi in Saurashtra, Gujarat. It was the home of Shri Chatrabhuj Bechar, the husband of Param Krupalu Dev's elder sister Shivkunvarben. Param Krupalu Dev frequently stayed at Chatrabhuj Bechar's home in Jetpar during His travels. It was here, in Bhadarva V.S. 1946 (1890 AD), that the first meeting between Shri Saubhagyabhai and Param Krupalu Dev took place — a meeting that would prove transformative for Saubhagyabhai, who became one of Param Krupalu Dev's most devoted disciples.

In Aso V.S. 1942 (1886 AD), while travelling to Mumbai for avdhan performances, Param Krupalu Dev stopped at Jetpar at his brother-in-law's home. During this visit, an astrologer named Shri Shankar Pancholi prepared a horoscope for Him. Later, in Aso V.S. 1943 (1887 AD), Shankar Pancholi witnessed Param Krupalu Dev demonstrate nashtavidya (lost knowledge recovery through astrology) in Jetpar and was left spellbound. Param Krupalu Dev also performed avdhans in Jetpar during this period.
