› Printing of Mokshmala and first meeting with Pujyashri Juthabhai

Main article: Mokshmala; Pujyashri Juthabhai
The peacock emblem of Ahmedabad, representing the city's name meaning "abode of peacocks".

At age 20, 1944 V.S (1888 AD) Param Krupalu Dev did not have money to publish Mokshmala. He had to collect money. Veraval's Nancybhai sponsored him some money. He goes to Ahmedabad as printing over there is cheaper than bombay. But Param Krupalu Dev did not know anyone in Ahmedabad. Vanechand Popatbhai Daftary said that he knows a person who is renowned and well established and will help you, in this way wrote a letter. The letter was written addressing Jaisinghbhai Ujamsingh, elder brother of Pujyashri Juthabhai which Param Krupalu Dev took and went to Ahmedabad, and which's content wrote that the person bringing in this letter (Param Krupalu Dev) is a really scholar person, is a Shatavdhani (and Shatavdhani that time had a seperate fan base) and please help him in any way you can. And now if a 20 year old kid comes to you, what happens? So he tells Param Krupalu Dev that he is very busy in his business and does not have time and tell his yonger brother Pujyashri Juthabhai to help him any way he can. Pujyashri Juthabhai lived only 2 years with him and then died but had attained Samkit. Total went to Ahmedabad for 2 months.

› Avdhans started

Main article: Avdhans

Param Krupalu Dev saw someone perform Ashtavdhan. The (family) took Param Krupalu Dev to see the Ashtavdhan getting performed so that he can get inspired or become hoshiyar. Shankarlal Bhatt performed the Ashtavdhan. Next day Param Krupalu Dev performs Ashtavdhan in Vasant Baug amonst his friends. For him it did not seem like a mojor heavy task. After that the word spread, that a 16 year old child performed Ashtavdhan unlike Shankarlal was was old and might have preppared for that a lot. Param Krupalu Dev did not know about Avdhan until yesterday and just by seeing it he could perform it the next day, such was his capabilities. And 2 days after this he did 12 Avdhans, in Upashray in front of 2000 people.

Param Krupalu Dev started school at age 7 in 1931 V.S. (1875 AD).

Showed extraordinary grasping power and memory.

His mamas staying in Rajkot used to recommend him to study law as he was so intelligent. They also suggested him to get English education in Rajkot (which Gandhiji got a chance to do - Alfred High School) as English school was not there in Ahmedabad. But Param Krupalu Dev was not interested in that as it would make him bahirmukh and he wanted to do his Atma's kalyan. And Param Krupalu Dev has seen Dr. Pranjivandas Jagjivan, that when he went to do medical abroad, all his manerism, food habits, eating habits and attitude changed. It took time for him to come back to indian way. and once he was back, Param Krupalu Dev praised that "have Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta sadai par aavi gaya chhe". All this Param Krupalu Dev could visualize.

Left/Completed school at age 11 in 1935 V.S. (1879 AD).
