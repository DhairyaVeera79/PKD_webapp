Limbdi

Significance Limbdi State provided guest house in Wadhwan Camp for Param Krupalu Dev's final stay
Period V.S. 1956–1957 (1900–1901 AD)

Limbdi is a town in the Surendranagar district of Gujarat, historically the seat of the princely Limbdi State. Limbdi's primary connection with Shrimad Rajchandra is through its neighbouring Wadhwan Camp, where the Limbdi State guest house served as Param Krupalu Dev's residence during the critical final period of His life in V.S. 1956–1957 (1900–1901 AD). It was at this guest house that Param Krupalu Dev founded the Paramshrut Prabhavak Mandal, dictated the 108 lessons of Pragnavbodh Mokshamala, and had His famous photographs taken.

› Limbdi State connection

From Shravan Vad 10, V.S. 1956 (1900 AD), when Param Krupalu Dev left Morbi for Wadhwan Camp, He stayed at the Limbdi State guest house. This became His base during some of the most significant events of His final years. After a journey to Ahmedabad and Mumbai, Param Krupalu Dev returned to the guest house on Maha Vad 6, V.S. 1957 (~1901 AD), by which time His body had diminished from approximately 130–140 lbs to just 57 lbs. He departed from Wadhwan Camp for Rajkot on Fagan Sud 6, V.S. 1957, where He would leave His mortal body.
