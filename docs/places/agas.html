Agas

Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram, Agas
Agas Ashram
Full name Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram, Agas
Region Gujarat, India (near Anand)
Established Jeth Sud 5, V.S. 1976 (~1920 AD)
Founder Lalluji Muni (Prabhushri)
Land 7.5 acre farm donated by Shri Jijibhai Kuberdas of Sandesar
Significance Primary ashram for Shrimad Rajchandra's spiritual legacy; site of Lalluji Muni's samadhi

Agas is a village near Anand in Gujarat, home to the Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram — the primary institution established to preserve and propagate Shrimad Rajchandra's spiritual legacy. The ashram was founded on Jeth Sud 5, V.S. 1976 (~1920 AD), nineteen years after Param Krupalu Dev's passing, by Lalluji Muni (also known as Prabhushri) on a 7.5-acre farm donated by Shri Jijibhai Kuberdas of Sandesar. Lalluji Muni spent fourteen monsoons at the ashram and passed away here on Vaishakh Sud 8, V.S. 1992 (~1936 AD), in an unprecedented state of samadhi. Brahmachariji Shri Govardhandasji was a key disciple who contributed significantly to the ashram's growth and the dissemination of Param Krupalu Dev's teachings.

› Establishment of the Ashram

On Jeth Sud 5, V.S. 1976 (~1920 AD), Lalluji Muni (Prabhushri) established the Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram in Agas. The land — a 7.5-acre farm — was donated by Shri Jijibhai Kuberdas of Sandesar, who offered it for the purpose of creating a spiritual retreat in Param Krupalu Dev's name.

The ashram was established nineteen years after Param Krupalu Dev's passing in V.S. 1957 (1901 AD). Lalluji Muni, who had been Param Krupalu Dev's closest monastic disciple and who had attained self-realisation under His guidance at Vaso in V.S. 1954 (1898 AD), chose this location in the Charotar region of Gujarat — the same area where Param Krupalu Dev had conducted many of His own retreats at Kavitha, Uttarsanda, and other villages.

› Lalluji Muni at Agas

Lalluji Muni spent fourteen monsoons at the Agas Ashram, making it the spiritual centre of the Shrimad Rajchandra tradition. Under his guidance, the ashram became a place of study, meditation, and the preservation of Param Krupalu Dev's letters, compositions, and teachings.

On Vaishakh Sud 8, V.S. 1992 (~1936 AD), Lalluji Muni passed away at the Agas Ashram at 8:10 PM, in what is described as an unprecedented samadhi. His passing at Agas further sanctified the ashram as a place of the highest spiritual significance in the tradition.

› Brahmachariji Govardhandasji

Brahmachariji Shri Govardhandasji was a key disciple at the Agas Ashram who played an important role in its development and the wider dissemination of Param Krupalu Dev's teachings. His dedication to the ashram and to the spiritual mission of preserving Shrimad Rajchandra's legacy helped establish Agas as a centre of living spiritual practice, not merely a memorial.

› Publications

The Paramshrut Prabhavak Mandal, founded by Param Krupalu Dev in Wadhwan Camp in V.S. 1956 (1900 AD), later published the second edition of Shrimad Rajchandra (the collected works) in V.S. 1982 (~1926 AD). This edition included a preface by Mahatma Gandhi, who had been deeply influenced by Param Krupalu Dev's teachings since their meeting in V.S. 1947 (1891 AD).

The first edition had been published earlier in V.S. 1961 (1905 AD) by Ambalalbhai and Mansukhbhai. The ashram at Agas continued this tradition of preserving and disseminating Param Krupalu Dev's written legacy.
