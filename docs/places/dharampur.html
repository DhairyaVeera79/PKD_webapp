Dharampur

Dharampur city skyline
Dharampur
Region South Gujarat, near Valsad
Significance Param Krupalu Dev visited for health recovery; home of son-in-law Bhagvanbhai Modi; site of Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram (est. 2001)
Period V.S. 1956 (1900 AD)
Key events Health retreat under Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta's care (V.S. 1956)

Dharampur, situated in south Gujarat near Valsad, is connected to Param Krupalu Dev's life in two ways: He retreated here in V.S. 1956 (1900 AD) for a change of weather as His health declined, and it was the native place of His son-in-law Shri Bhagvanbhai Modi. In modern times, Dharampur is home to the Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram, a 223-acre spiritual campus on the hillock of Mohangadh, founded by Pujya Gurudevshri Rakeshji in 2001.

› Param Krupalu Dev's visit (V.S. 1956)

In Chaitra V.S. 1956 (1900 AD), Param Krupalu Dev's body was struck by what was diagnosed as colitis, and His health was deteriorating. He retreated to Dharampur for a change of weather and was treated by Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta, who was both His wife Jhabakbai's brother and a trusted doctor. After spending time here, He continued onwards, visiting Ahmedabad, Viramgam, Vavania (for about 2 months), and Morbi before eventually proceeding to Wadhwan.

› Bhagvanbhai Modi

Shri Bhagvanbhai Modi, who married Param Krupalu Dev's first daughter Javalben, was from Dharampur. Through the Modi family, Dharampur maintained a continued connection to Param Krupalu Dev's legacy. Bhagvanbhai and Javalben later played a role in establishing a memorial at Vavania, Param Krupalu Dev's birthplace.
