Botad is a town in Saurashtra, Gujarat. In approximately V.S. 1940 (1884 AD), Shrimad Rajchandra performed 52 avdhans (simultaneous attention demonstrations) in Botad in the presence of Sheth Shri Harilal Shivlal. This was among the most extensive avdhan demonstrations performed by Param Krupalu Dev during the period when He was showcasing this extraordinary power across Saurashtra, including performances in Morbi, Jamnagar, Wadhwan, Jetpar, and Ahmedabad.

Main article: Avdhans

The 52 simultaneous avdhans performed in Botad were among the highest numbers demonstrated by Param Krupalu Dev in any single sitting outside of Mumbai, where He would later perform the famous Shatavdhan (100 avdhans). Sheth Shri Harilal Shivlal, a prominent figure in Botad, was present to witness the demonstration. The performance cemented Param Krupalu Dev's growing reputation as a prodigy of extraordinary mental powers.
