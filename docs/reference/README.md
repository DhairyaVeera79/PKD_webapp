## Book Structure (14 Chapters + Appendices)

1. **Introduction** – Overview of Shrimad's multifaceted personality
2. **Birth and Early Childhood** – Birth in Vavania, family background, early years
3. **Recollection of Past Lives (Jatismaranjnan)** – At age 7, triggered by witnessing a funeral
4. **Educational Advancement and Religious Impressions of Childhood** – Schooling, intellectual prowess, early spiritual inclinations
5. **Religious Churning** – Ages 13-16, study of six philosophies, adoption of Jainism
6. **Extraordinary Powers Manifested in Youth** – Avdhanshakti, Astrology, Miraculous Powers
7. **A Householder's Life** – Marriage, family life, detachment within matrimony
8. **Business** – Jewellery business partnership, ethical business conduct
9. **Attainment of Self-Realisation** – Shuddh Samkit in V.S. 1947
10. **Samadhi Amidst Worldly Affairs** – V.S. 1947 to V.S. 1951
11. **Intense Sadhana During Spiritual Retreats** – V.S. 1952 to V.S. 1957, retreats in Kavitha, Vaso, Uttarsanda, Kheda, Idar
12. **The Final Stage of Shrimad's Life** – Illness, last days, passing in Rajkot
13. **Shrimad's Devotees** – Juthabhai, Saubhagyabhai, Ambalalbhai, Lalluji Muni, Gandhiji
14. **Epilogue** – Legacy and impact

**Appendices:**
- An Introduction to the Mission (Shrimad Rajchandra Mission Dharampur)
- Institutions within India and outside India associated with Shrimad
- Chronological Representation of Shrimad's Life
- Footnotes/References

## Key Terminology

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| V.S. | Vikram Samvat (Indian calendar; V.S. year ≈ A.D. year + 57) |
| Param Krupalu Dev | Title meaning "Supremely Compassionate Lord" – used for Shrimad |
| Samyagdarshan / Samkit | Right belief / Self-realisation |
| Shuddh Samkit | Pure right belief through direct self-experience |
| Jatismaranjnan | Recollection of past lives |
| Avdhanshakti | Power of retention / simultaneous attention to multiple tasks |
| Shatavdhani | One who can simultaneously attend to 100 tasks |
| Sadguru | True spiritual master |
| Mumukshu | Seeker of liberation |
| Paryushan | Annual Jain festival of spiritual observance |
| Patrank | Letter number (from Shrimad's collected letters) |
| Samadhi | State of deep spiritual absorption |
| Kevaljnan | Omniscience |
| Moksha | Liberation from the cycle of birth and death |
