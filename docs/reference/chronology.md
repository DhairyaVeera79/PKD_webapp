## Note on Dates
- Traditional birth date in A.D. is believed to be November 9, 1867. However, based on modern research in the book, it is reported as **November 10, 1867** (confirmed that Kartik Purnima of V.S. 1924 was a Sunday, November 10, 1867 A.D.)
- The book notes Shrimad was born on Kartik Purnima, same day as Kalikaalsarvajna Acharyashri Hemchandrasuriji (V.S. 1145, ~800 years earlier) and Guru Nanak (founder of Sikh religion).
