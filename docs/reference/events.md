### Jatismaranjnan (Recollection of Past Lives)
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Date** | V.S. 1931 (circa 1874 A.D.) |
| **Age** | 7 years old |
| **Trigger** | Death of Shri Amichandbhai by snakebite in Vavania. Shrimad witnessed the cremation. |
| **What happened** | While observing the funeral pyre, Shrimad was overwhelmed by the mystery of death and life. In that instant, knowledge of countless past lives flooded His consciousness. |
| **Effect** | Could recall details of previous births with perfect clarity. Later told Kalyanjibhai He could recollect 800 previous births. Told Padamshi Thakarshi (at Digambar Jain temple in Mumbai) about the exact moment and circumstance. |
| **Significance** | Extremely rare spiritual attainment. Confirmed Shrimad as an extraordinary soul. Became the foundation of His unshakeable conviction about the soul's existence, eternity, and transmigration. |

## 2. Avdhan (Concentration) Events

### Early Avdhans in Morbi
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Period** | V.S. 1937-1940 (age ~13-16) |
| **Inspiration** | Witnessed Shastri Shankarlal Maheshwar Bhatt (Shankar Pancholi) perform 8 avdhans in Morbi |
| **Shrimad's response** | Began practicing and quickly surpassed all known demonstrations |
| **Types** | Avadhani (8-fold), Ashtavadhani (8), Shodashavadhani (16), Shatavadhani (100) |

### Shatavdhan at Framjee Cawasjee Institute, Mumbai
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Date** | Maha Vad 13, V.S. 1943 (January 22, 1887 A.D.) |
| **Location** | Framjee Cawasjee Institute, Dhobi Talao, Mumbai |
| **Age** | 19 years old |
| **What it was** | Keeping track of 100 different tasks simultaneously while answering questions, composing poetry, playing games, solving mathematics, etc. |
| **Presided by** | Dr. Peterson (European scholar) |
| **Result** | Complete success. Dr. Peterson awarded Shrimad a gold medal. |
| **Aftermath** | Media sensation. Invited to Europe by Sir Charles Sergeant (Chief Magistrate of Mumbai High Court) – Shrimad declined. Earned title "Shatavadhani" and recognition as extraordinary genius. |

### Avdhans at Theosophical Society, Mumbai
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Location** | Theosophical Society, Girgaum, Mumbai |
| **Notable** | Shri Rustamji Ardeshar Master presented Shrimad with garland and bouquet |

### Other Avdhan Venues
| Place | Count / Details |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Ahmedabad** (Dalpatbhai's compound) | 52 avdhans (V.S. 1945) |
| **Wadhwan Camp** | 16 avdhans (Colonel H. L. Natsaheb present) |
| **Botad** | 52 avdhans (Sheth Harilal Shivlal present) |
| **Various places across Gujarat** | Numerous demonstrations in V.S. 1940-1945 period |

### Shrimad's Renunciation of Avdhan Powers
| Detail | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **When** | Around V.S. 1945-1946, Shrimad deliberately stopped performing avdhans |
| **Why** | Recognized these powers, though extraordinary, were obstacles to the ultimate goal of self-realisation. Called them "tinsel" compared to the "gold" of spiritual attainment. Felt they had served their purpose (establishing credibility) and were now a distraction. |
| **Quote** | Expressed that these were worldly feats unworthy of a soul seeking liberation |

## 8. Meeting with Gandhiji

### First Meeting
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Date** | Jeth, V.S. 1947 (May/June 1891) |
| **Location** | Home of Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta, Mumbai |
| **Context** | Gandhiji had just returned from England as a barrister. Dr. Pranjivandas introduced them. |
| **Impact** | Gandhiji was deeply impressed. Began visiting Shrimad's office daily for 2 years. Called Shrimad his foremost guide (among Shrimad, Ruskin, and Tolstoy). |

### The 27 Questions
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **When** | After Gandhiji went to South Africa (V.S. 1949 onwards) |
| **Context** | Gandhiji was in spiritual crisis; considering Christianity and Islam. Sent 27 questions about soul, karma, liberation, etc. |
| **Shrimad's response** | Answered each question with lucid logic and deep spiritual insight. Recommended five books. |
| **Impact** | Prevented Gandhiji from converting; deepened his understanding of Indian spiritual tradition |

### Gandhiji's Post-Shrimad Activities
| Event | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Celibacy vow** | V.S. 1962, Phoenix, South Africa; inspired by Shrimad's composition 'Nirkhine Navyauvana' |
| **Phoenix Ashram** | V.S. 1960, near Durban |
| **Preface** | Wrote preface for 2nd edition of 'Shrimad Rajchandra' |
| **Autobiography chapter** | 'Raychandbhai' chapter in his autobiography |
| **Annual celebration** | Celebrated Shrimad Rajchandra Jayanti on Kartik Purnima at Kochrab/Sabarmati Ashram |

### Passing of Shrimad Rajchandra
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Date** | Chaitra Vad 5, V.S. 1957 (April 9, 1901 A.D.) |
| **Time** | 2:00 PM (Tuesday) |
| **Place** | Rajkot (Fakirchand Chawl / Brahmakshatriya Mohalla) |
| **Age** | 33 years (same age as Jesus Christ, as noted in the book) |
| **Physical state** | Extremely emaciated from prolonged fasting and austerities; tuberculosis/consumption |
| **Final hours** | On Chaitra Vad 4 (day before), told devotees: "Do not worry, this Soul is eternal." At 2:30 AM caught severe cold; said "Your brother's death will be in the state of samadhi." At 7:30 AM asked to be moved to a couch for meditative posture. At 7:45 AM accepted milk. At 8:45 AM told Mansukhbhai: "I am now abiding in the Self." From 7:45 AM to 2:00 PM remained in absolute state of samadhi; face glowed ever more radiantly as life force receded. At 2:00 PM, the Pure Soul severed its ties with the body. |
| **Last words** | "Aum Aum Aum" |
| **Present** | Mansukhbhai (brother), Navalchandbhai, Narbherambhai, and others |
| **Radiance** | Navalchandbhai wrote to Ambalalbhai that Shrimad's face was extraordinarily radiant at death – more luminous than ever before |
| **Will** | Willed assets to brother Mansukhbhai |
