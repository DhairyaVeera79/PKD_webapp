## Primary Places (Major Significance)

### Vavania (Vavania-mota)
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Also known as** | Vavania-mota (to distinguish from a smaller village of the same name) |
| **District** | Morbi (Saurashtra / Kathiawar, Gujarat) |
| **Significance** | Shrimad's birthplace and childhood home. Ravjibhai's family settled here from V.S. 1892. Shrimad lived here until ~V.S. 1937. |
| **Key locations** | Ravjibhai's house; local school; Vaishnav haveli; cremation ground (where Amichandbhai's funeral triggered jatismaranjnan) |
| **Events** | Birth of Shrimad (Kartik Purnima V.S. 1924); jatismaranjnan (V.S. 1931); early childhood education; many intuitiveness incidents |

### Morbi
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Region** | Saurashtra, Gujarat |
| **Significance** | Shrimad's schooling (V.S. 1937–1940). Avdhan experiments began here. Place of marriage (V.S. 1944). |
| **Key locations** | Anglo-Vernacular school; Darbar Saheb's office |
| **Events** | 8 avdhans by Shri Shankar Pancholi witnessed by Shrimad; Shrimad's own first avdhans; Darbar Saheb invited Shrimad for examinations; marriage at Morbi |
| **People connected** | Shri Mansukhbhai Kiratchand Mehta; Shri Chhotalal Revashankar Anjaria; Shri Vanechand Daftari |

### Mumbai (Bombay)
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Significance** | Where Shrimad lived and conducted business from ~V.S. 1945 onwards. Site of famous shatavdhan. Met Gandhiji here. Revashankar Jagjivan Co. headquarters. |
| **Key locations** | Framjee Cawasjee Institute (Dhobi Talao); Theosophical Society (Girgaum); Shrimad's office/shop (Kalbadevi); Digambar Jain temple (near Bhuleshwar); Dr. Pranjivandas's residence |
| **Events** | Shatavdhan (January 22, 1887 / Maha Vad 13, V.S. 1943); avdhans at Theosophical Society; 52 avdhans at Sheth Dalpatbhai's compound; jatismaranjnan recollection at Digambar Jain temple; meeting Gandhiji (V.S. 1947); jewellery business established |
| **People connected** | Dr. Peterson; Sir Charles Sergeant; Maneklal Ghelabhai; Revashankarbhai Jagjivan; Gandhiji; Padamshi Thakarshi |

### Rajkot
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Region** | Saurashtra, Gujarat |
| **Significance** | Site of Shrimad's passing. Memorial (samadhi sthal) built here. |
| **Key locations** | Samadhi Sthal (memorial); residence where Shrimad spent final days (Fakirchand Chawl / Brahmakshatriya Mohalla) |
| **Events** | Shrimad's passing – Chaitra Vad 5, V.S. 1957 (April 9, 1901) at 7:17 PM; Gandhiji's first visit to memorial after Shrimad's death |
| **People connected** | Mansukhbhai (brother); Navalchandbhai; Narbherambhai |
| **Gandhiji connection** | Gandhiji celebrated Shrimad Rajchandra Jayanti here; went directly to Shrimad's memorial from railway station on first visit. |

### Khambhat (Cambay)
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Region** | Gujarat |
| **Significance** | Ambalalbhai's native place. Site where Lalluji Muni was initiated as monk (V.S. 1940). Lalluji Muni first met Shrimad here. |
| **Key locations** | Upashray (monks' dwelling); Jain shrines; Sthanakvasi seat |
| **Events** | Lalluji Muni's initiation; First meeting of Shrimad and Lalluji Muni on Dhanteras V.S. 1946; Ambalalbhai's death (Chaitra Vad 12, V.S. 1963) |
| **People connected** | Ambalalbhai Lalchand; Lalluji Muni; Devkaranji Muni; Chhotalal Manekchand; Tribhuvanbhai |

### Kavitha (Kawitha)
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Region** | Gujarat (near Nadiad, Charotar region) |
| **Significance** | Site of Shrimad's first major spiritual retreat (V.S. 1952). He spent Paryushan here. Shrimad's earliest outward signs of deep renunciation. |
| **Key locations** | Jhaverchand Sheth's residence; Vagadia lake; fields near the village; Mahadev mandir |
| **Events** | Paryushan retreat; nightly meditations by Vagadia lake; incident with bandit Babardeva; Shrimad wandered alone in dark of night; search party with lanterns by Ratanchand & Venichand; Shamalbhai bringing jasmine flowers |
| **People connected** | Shri Jhaverchand Sheth; Shri Kunwarjibhai; Shri Laherabhai; Shri Vanmalibhai; Shri Jhaverbhai; Shri Vrajbhai Gangadas Patel; Shamalbhai; Babardeva; Ratanchand; Venichand; Lallubhai (caretaker) |

### Uttarsanda (Untarsanda)
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Region** | Gujarat (Charotar region, near Anand) |
| **Significance** | Site of Shrimad's intense spiritual retreat (2nd Paryushan in retreat, V.S. 1953). Was in elevated spiritual state manifesting extraordinary radiance. |
| **Key locations** | Small bungalow (found by Motilal Bhavsar); open grounds; pipal tree |
| **Events** | Deep meditation retreats; Shrimad's ecstatic states; spiritual dialogues; Motilal Bhavsar's wife Navalben told she would attain liberation in 8th life |
| **People connected** | Shri Motilal Bhavsar; Navalben; Shri Laherabhai |

### Nadiad
| Attribute | Detail |

| **Significance** | Near Kavitha; part of the area where Shrimad's retreats occurred. |

### Jetpar
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Region** | Saurashtra, Gujarat |
| **Significance** | Home of Shri Chatrabhuj Bechar (Shivkunvarben's husband, Shrimad's brother-in-law). Shrimad frequently stayed here. First meeting of Saubhagyabhai with Shrimad took place here (Bhadarva V.S. 1946). |
| **Events** | First meeting of Saubhagyabhai and Shrimad; Shrimad's frequent visits and stays |
| **People connected** | Shri Chatrabhuj Bechar; Shivkunvarben; Saubhagyabhai |

### Sayla (Shayla)
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Region** | Saurashtra, Gujarat |
| **Significance** | Called 'the village of the devotees'. Home of Saubhagyabhai Lallubhai. Also home of Jijiben (married to Jhaverchand Malukchand of Sayla). |
| **People connected** | Saubhagyabhai Lallubhai; Dungarshibhai Gosalia; Jijiben; Jhaverchand Malukchand |

### Idar
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Region** | North Gujarat |
| **Significance** | Site of Shrimad's final spiritual retreat. Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta was Chief Medical Officer of Idar State. Shrimad stayed at Dr. Pranjivandas's residence. Explored hills and caves. |
| **Key locations** | Dr. Pranjivandas's residence; caves in the hills; forests |
| **Events** | Shrimad's retreat in V.S. 1955/1956; exploration of caves; intense meditation and vairagya; Shrimad's deeply emaciated spiritual state. Overheard telling Devkaranji about His spiritual progression. |
| **People connected** | Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta; Devkaranji Muni; Thakarshi (Saubhagyabhai's nephew) |

### Wadhwan Camp
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Region** | Surendranagar district, Gujarat |
| **Significance** | Site where the famous photographs of Shrimad were taken. Also where 16 avdhans were performed. |
| **Key locations** | Photo studio |
| **Events** | 16 avdhans performed (Colonel H. L. Natsaheb present); photographs taken during state of extreme dispassion. Shrimad instructed Sukhlal Chaganlal to get photographs taken. Later told Mansukhbhai those photographs captured His state like Parshwanath during Kamath's torment. |
| **People connected** | Sukhlal Chaganlal; Mansukhbhai Devshi; Colonel H. L. Natsaheb |

### Agas
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Region** | Gujarat (near Anand) |
| **Full name** | Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram, Agas |
| **Established** | Jeth Sud 5, V.S. 1976 (founded by Lalluji Muni / Prabhushri) |
| **Land** | 7.5 acre farm donated by Shri Jijibhai Kuberdas of Sandesar |
| **Significance** | Primary ashram for Shrimad Rajchandra's spiritual legacy, established 19 years after His passing. Lalluji Muni spent 14 monsoons here. Brahmachariji Shri Govardhandasji was key disciple here. Lalluji Muni passed away here V.S. 1992. |
| **People connected** | Lalluji Muni (Prabhushri); Brahmachariji Shri Govardhandasji; Jijibhai Kuberdas |

### Dharampur
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Region** | South Gujarat, near Valsad |
| **Significance** | Site of Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram (est. 2001) founded by Pujya Gurudevshri Rakeshbhai. 223 acres on hillock of Mohangadh. Houses temples, meditation halls, educational buildings, hospital. |
| **Key locations** | Shrimad Rajchandra Ashram (223 acres); Shri Dharmanath Tirthdhaam (Jain temple); Pujya Gurudevshri's meditation spot |
