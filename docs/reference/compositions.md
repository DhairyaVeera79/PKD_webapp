## Works by Shrimad Rajchandra

### Major Compositions

#### 1. Shri Atmasiddhi Shastra (The Six Fundamentals / Self-Realisation)
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Full title** | Shri Atmasiddhi Shastra |
| **Form** | Philosophical poem; 142 verses (stanzas) |
| **Language** | Gujarati |
| **Composed** | Aso Vad Ekam (Aso Vad 1), V.S. 1952 (circa October 1896), in approximately 1.5 to 2 hours |
| **Location** | Nadiad (while staying with Saubhagyabhai's group) |
| **Occasion** | Composed for Saubhagyabhai Lallubhai, who was Shrimad's divine soulmate |
| **Structure** | Dialogue form between a Guru (enlightened preceptor) and a Shishya (seeker). Establishes six fundamental truths (Shad Darshan): (1) Soul exists, (2) Soul is eternal, (3) Soul is the doer of its actions, (4) Soul is the receiver of consequences, (5) Liberation exists, (6) Path to liberation exists |
| **Amanuensis** | Ambalalbhai wrote it down while Shrimad dictated; held a lantern for light |
| **Significance** | Crown jewel of Shrimad's compositions; encapsulates the essence of all scriptures; Pujya Gurudevshri Rakeshbhai's Ph.D. thesis was on this work |
| **Key verse (opening)** | 'Aatma chhe, te nit chhe, karta bhokta soy; moksha chhe, te nu karan, samakit te hoy' |

#### 2. Mokshmala (Garland of Liberation)
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Full title** | Mokshmala |
| **Also called** | 'Pragnavbodh Mokshamala' (alternate manuscript title) |
| **Form** | Prose – collection of essays on spiritual topics |
| **Published** | V.S. 1944 (1887-88), in Ahmedabad |
| **Age when written** | Around 16 years old |
| **Chapters** | 108 pads (essays/topics) |
| **Recommended by Shrimad to** | Gandhiji (among books to study) |
| **Significance** | First published work. Shows extraordinary spiritual maturity at young age. Covers diverse topics including nature of soul, karma, right conduct, liberation. Juthabhai helped with its publication. |

### The Compilation 'Shrimad Rajchandra'
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Nature** | Posthumous compilation of Shrimad's letters, poems, prose, and philosophical writings |
| **First edition** | V.S. 1961, compiled by Ambalalbhai and Mansukhbhai |
| **Second edition** | Compiled later; Gandhiji wrote the preface (requested by Revashankarbhai) |
| **Contents** | ~920 letters, numerous poems, prose pieces, philosophical discourses |
| **Structure** | Letters organized chronologically; compositions appended; includes autobiography notes |
| **Significance** | Primary source material for all study of Shrimad Rajchandra. The book under study draws heavily from this compilation. |

## Key Details about Shrimad's Writing

- **Age of prolific writing**: Majority of compositions written between ages 16-33 (V.S. 1940-1957)
- **Languages**: Primarily Gujarati; also proficient in Sanskrit, Hindi, Prakrit
- **Writing style**: Described as effortless; compositions flowed like a fountain without corrections
- **Total literary output**: Compositions, ~920 letters, prose works, commentaries, notes
- **Atmasiddhi Shastra timing**: 142 verses in ~1.5 hours – demonstrating extraordinary creative/spiritual capacity
- **Last known writing**: During Idar retreat and final months in Rajkot (V.S. 1956-57)
