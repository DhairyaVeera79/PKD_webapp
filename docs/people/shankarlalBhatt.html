Shastri Shankarlal Maheshwar Bhatt

Shastri Shankarlal Maheshwar Bhatt
Shastri Shankarlal Maheshwar Bhatt
Known for Performed 8 avdhans in Morbi (V.S. 1940)
Significance Param Krupalu Dev attended a viewing of his eight avdhans, then performed the feat Himself the next day

Shastri Shankarlal Maheshwar Bhatt performed eight avdhans in Morbi. At that time, as per available records, only two people in all of India exhibited such a feat — Shastri Shankarlal and Shri Gattulalji Maharaj, who performed eight avdhans in Mumbai.

In V.S. 1940, around the time Param Krupalu Dev arrived in Morbi, a viewing of Shastri Shankarlal's eight avdhans had been organised in the sacred Jain shelter for monks. Param Krupalu Dev — then known as Vanikbhushan Kavishwar, the merchant poet — was invited to attend. Grasping the technique immediately upon watching the performance, He repeated the feat the very next day in Morbi's Vasant Garden before a few friends, performing a different set of eight tasks. The following day, in the same shelter, He successfully performed 12 avdhans to an audience of 2000 people.

Not to be confused with Shri Shankar Pancholi, the astrologer of Jetpar who cast Param Krupalu Dev's question-based horoscope and was later left spellbound by His nashtavidya demonstration. They are two different people.
