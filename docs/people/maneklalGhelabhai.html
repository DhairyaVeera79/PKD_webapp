Shri Maneklal Ghelabhai

Shri Maneklal Ghelabhai
Shri Maneklal Ghelabhai
Also known as Makubhai
Native place Vadodara
Expertise Jewellery valuation (father was expert in precious stones)
First meeting Shatavdhan in Mumbai, V.S. 1943
Role Business partner in Revashankar Jagjivan Company
Significance Taught Param Krupalu Dev jewellery valuation; key business partner

Shri Maneklal Ghelabhai (also known as Makubhai) of Vadodara was one of Param Krupalu Dev's close associates and a founding partner of the firm Revashankar Jagjivan ni Pedhi. His father was an authority on precious stone valuation. Maneklal Ghelabhai was first drawn to Param Krupalu Dev upon witnessing the shatavdhan (100-fold concentration) performance in Mumbai in V.S. 1943. He subsequently taught Param Krupalu Dev the art of jewellery valuation, which enabled the founding of the jewellery business.

› Meeting at shatavdhan

Shri Maneklal Ghelabhai attended Param Krupalu Dev's shatavdhan performance in Mumbai in V.S. 1943. He was deeply impressed by Param Krupalu Dev's extraordinary powers of concentration and memory. After the demonstration, the two had a conversation in which Param Krupalu Dev expressed that He needed to earn some money but did not know how. Maneklal Ghelabhai, recognising Param Krupalu Dev's exceptional calibre, offered to teach Him the art of jewellery valuation — a skill that his own father had mastered as an expert in precious stones.

› Jewellery expertise

Maneklal Ghelabhai's family had deep expertise in the jewellery trade — his father was recognised as an authority on the valuation of precious stones. This knowledge was passed on to Param Krupalu Dev, who quickly mastered the trade. This expertise became the foundation for the jewellery business that Param Krupalu Dev, Revashankarbhai, and Maneklal Ghelabhai would later establish together.

› Business partnership

Maneklal Ghelabhai became one of the three founding partners of the firm Revashankar Jagjivan ni Pedhi in Mumbai. The partnership combined the strengths of all three: Shri Revashankarbhai Jagjivan provided the capital, Maneklal Ghelabhai contributed his expert knowledge of jewellery and precious stone valuation, and Param Krupalu Dev managed the company with perfect ethical conduct.
