› Composition of Mokshmala

The peacock emblem of Morbi, representing the city's name meaning "abode of peacocks".

Mokshmala was composed by Param Krupalu Dev at age 16 years 5 months in 3 days at house of Vinaychandrabhai Popatbhai Daftary in Chaitra month of 1940 V.S (1884 AD). 108 chapters. There are stories from Jin Agams (most from Uttaradhyayana sutra); Dos/Donts; philosophical talks. 3 sections: Samyag darshan, Samyag Jnan, Samyag Charitra. And the overall length of chapters is very concise.

› Printing of Mokshmala

The peacock emblem of Ahmedabad, representing the city's name meaning "abode of peacocks".

At age 20, 1944 V.S (1888 AD) Param Krupalu Dev did not have money to publish Mokshmala. He had to collect money. Veraval's Nancybhai sponsored him some money. He goes to Ahmedabad as printing over there is cheaper than bombay. But Param Krupalu Dev did not know anyone in Ahmedabad. Vanechand Popatbhai Daftary said that he knows a person who is renowned and well established and will help you, in this way wrote a letter. The letter was written addressing Jaisinghbhai Ujamsingh, elder brother of Pujyashri Juthabhai which Param Krupalu Dev took and went to Ahmedabad, and which's content wrote that the person bringing in this letter (Param Krupalu Dev) is a really scholar person, is a Shatavdhani (and Shatavdhani that time had a seperate fan base) and please help him in any way you can. And now if a 20 year old kid comes to you, what happens? So he tells Param Krupalu Dev that he is very busy in his business and does not have time and tell his yonger brother Pujyashri Juthabhai to help him any way he can. And that is how Param Krupalu Dev met Pujyashri Juthabhai for the first time in this lifetime.

› Name change of Param Krupalu Dev

Param Krupalu Dev's name was changed from Laxminandan/Abhaychand to Raichand at Age 4 in 1928 V.S. (1871 AD). For whatever reason the name change happened, maybe the name change was prompted by Bhagvan himself because for a lot many of his previous births, his name was Raichand only (Sanskrit Tanslation: Rajchandra). Param Kruplau Dev's previous birth - Nepal / Tibet 's Rajkuvar. He had 2 wives, and one of them now in Param Krupalu Dev's birth is Juthabhai. 2nd wife which is now in Param Krupalu Dev birth - Chanchalben. And now in the birth previos to this Nepal's Rajkuvar birth, Param Krupalu Dev was a Digamber Sadhu in Idar and has left his body over there because someone had poisoned him because he was extraordinary scholar of Jainism. He got to know he has got poisoned so he got in Kayotsarg and left his body.

› Schooling

The Machhu River flowing through Pujyashri Juthabhai, a vital water source for the region.

Param Krupalu Dev started school at age 7 in 1931 V.S. (1875 AD).

Showed extraordinary grasping power and memory.

His mamas staying in Rajkot used to recommend him to study law as he was so intelligent. They also suggested him to get English education in Rajkot (which Gandhiji got a chance to do - Alfred High School) as English school was not there in Pujyashri Juthabhai. But Param Krupalu Dev was not interested in that as it would make him bahirmukh and he wanted to do his Atma's kalyan. And Param Krupalu Dev has seen Dr. Pranjivandas Jagjivan, that when he went to do medical abroad, all his manerism, food habits, eating habits and attitude changed. It took time for him to come back to indian way. and once he was back, Param Krupalu Dev praised that "have Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta sadai par aavi gaya chhe". All this Param Krupalu Dev could visualize.

Left/Completed school at age 11 in 1935 V.S. (1879 AD).
