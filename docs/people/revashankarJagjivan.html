Shri Revashankarbhai Jagjivan was Param Krupalu Dev's wife's uncle — the younger brother of Shri Popatlalbhai Mehta (Param Krupalu Dev's father-in-law) — and one of Param Krupalu Dev's closest associates. Previously a lawyer in Morbi, he moved to Mumbai in Ashadh V.S. 1945 on Param Krupalu Dev's counsel, who predicted that the jewellery business would be far more profitable for him. He co-founded the firm Revashankar Jagjivan ni Pedhi with Param Krupalu Dev and Shri Maneklal Ghelabhai. After Param Krupalu Dev's passing, he served as chief executive of the Paramshrut Prabhavak Mandal and was instrumental in the publication of Param Krupalu Dev's works, including requesting Gandhiji to write the preface for the 2nd edition of 'Shrimad Rajchandra'.

› Early career in Morbi

Shri Revashankarbhai was a lawyer based in Morbi. During Param Krupalu Dev's deepening of studies into astrology, palmistry, and physiognomy — disciplines in which He could determine a person's birth year, birth time, and future simply by looking at their face or palm — Param Krupalu Dev told Revashankarbhai that his career in law would not prosper. Instead, Param Krupalu Dev advised him to move to Mumbai and enter the jewellery business, predicting great success.

Acting on this counsel, Revashankarbhai left Morbi and moved to Mumbai in Ashadh V.S. 1945, bringing whatever capital he had.

› Revashankar Jagjivan Company

The firm Revashankar Jagjivan ni Pedhi was established in Mumbai. It was named after Shri Revashankarbhai as he provided the primary funding. The three partners were:

  • Shri Revashankarbhai Jagjivan — the financier
  • Shri Maneklal Ghelabhai — who possessed expert knowledge of jewellery valuation (his father was an authority on precious stones)
  • Param Krupalu Dev — who managed the company with perfect ethical conduct

Revashankarbhai recognised that Param Krupalu Dev could manage the business wonderfully due to His perfection in all matters. The business required frequent travel to Surat, Bharuch, and Ahmedabad. During one such trip, Param Krupalu Dev met Shri Naginchand Kapurchand, a jeweller from Surat who joined the firm from V.S. 1948. Shri Chhotalal Lallubhai of Ahmedabad also joined around the same time.

Before the company was established, Param Krupalu Dev had been staying in the basement of Shri Tribhuvanbhai Manekchand's home in Santacruz. Once the business began, the commute became impractical, so Param Krupalu Dev moved to Girgaon to be closer to the firm.

› Mani Bhavan

The residence of the Jagjivan brothers in Mumbai was Mani Bhavan. This building later became Mahatma Gandhiji's Mumbai headquarters for about 17 years (1917–1934), with Revashankarbhai serving as Gandhiji's friend and host during this period. It was from Mani Bhavan that Gandhiji initiated the Non-Cooperation, Satyagraha, Swadeshi, Khadi, and Khilafat movements.

› Paramshrut Prabhavak Mandal

After Param Krupalu Dev's passing, Shri Revashankarbhai served as chief executive of the Paramshrut Prabhavak Mandal — the organisation dedicated to preserving and disseminating Param Krupalu Dev's teachings and writings. In this capacity, he requested Gandhiji to write the preface for the 2nd edition of 'Shrimad Rajchandra', which was published in V.S. 1982. Gandhiji agreed, and his preface became a landmark document in the recognition of Param Krupalu Dev's spiritual legacy.
