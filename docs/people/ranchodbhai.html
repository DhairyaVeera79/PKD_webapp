Shri Ranchodbhai
Native place Dharampur
Relation Father of Shri Bhagvanbhai Modi (Javalben's husband)

Shri Ranchodbhai was from Dharampur and was the father of Shri Bhagvanbhai Modi, who married Param Krupalu Dev's first daughter Smt. Javalben.
