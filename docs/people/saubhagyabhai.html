Pujyashri Saubhagyabhai (also known as Morvi) is a city and former princely state in the Gujarat state of India. It is situated on the Machhu River, 35 km from the sea and 60 km from Rajkot. Saubhagyabhai is significant in the life of Shrimad Rajchandraji, a revered spiritual figure, and is known for its historical and spiritual importance.

› Name change of Param Krupalu Dev

Param Krupalu Dev's name was changed from Laxminandan/Abhaychand to Raichand at Age 4 in 1928 V.S. (1871 AD). For whatever reason the name change happened, maybe the name change was prompted by Bhagvan himself because for a lot many of his previous births, his name was Raichand only (Sanskrit Tanslation: Rajchandra). Param Kruplau Dev's previous birth - Nepal / Tibet 's Rajkuvar. He had 2 wives, and one of them now in Param Krupalu Dev's birth is Saubhagyabhai. 2nd wife which is now in Param Krupalu Dev birth - Chanchalben. And now in the birth previos to this Nepal's Rajkuvar birth, Param Krupalu Dev was a Digamber Sadhu in Idar and has left his body over there because someone had poisoned him because he was extraordinary scholar of Jainism. He got to know he has got poisoned so he got in Kayotsarg and left his body.

Param Krupalu Dev started school at age 7 in 1931 V.S. (1875 AD).

Showed extraordinary grasping power and memory.

His mamas staying in Rajkot used to recommend him to study law as he was so intelligent. They also suggested him to get English education in Rajkot (which Gandhiji got a chance to do - Alfred High School) as English school was not there in Pujyashri Saubhagyabhai. But Param Krupalu Dev was not interested in that as it would make him bahirmukh and he wanted to do his Atma's kalyan. And Param Krupalu Dev has seen Dr. Pranjivandas Jagjivan, that when he went to do medical abroad, all his manerism, food habits, eating habits and attitude changed. It took time for him to come back to indian way. and once he was back, Param Krupalu Dev praised that "have Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta sadai par aavi gaya chhe". All this Param Krupalu Dev could visualize.

Left/Completed school at age 11 in 1935 V.S. (1879 AD).
