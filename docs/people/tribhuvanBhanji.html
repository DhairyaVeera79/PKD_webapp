Shri Tribhuvanbhai Manekchand

Shri Tribhuvanbhai Manekchand
Shri Tribhuvanbhai Manekchand
Native place Khambhat
Father Shri Manekchandbhai
Relation Friend of Ambalalbhai
Significance Accompanied Ambalalbhai to meet Param Krupalu Dev in Mumbai; Param Krupalu Dev stayed at his Santacruz home

Shri Tribhuvanbhai Manekchand was a friend of Ambalalbhai from Khambhat, and the son of Shri Manekchandbhai. He accompanied Ambalalbhai to meet Param Krupalu Dev in Mumbai in V.S. 1945. When Param Krupalu Dev first came to Mumbai in search of business, He stayed for six months in the basement of Tribhuvanbhai's home in Santacruz.
