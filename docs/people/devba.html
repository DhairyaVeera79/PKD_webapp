Smt. Devba (also called Devbai) was the mother of Param Krupalu Dev and the wife of Shri Ravjibhai. She was the daughter of Shri Raghavjibhai of Maliya and was born into a Jain family. Even after her marriage into the Vaishnav household of Ravjibhai in Vavania, she continued to follow the Jain way of life. She was described as simple, cultured, and an epitome of good character, loving kindness and humility. Soft-hearted by nature, her eyes would well up at the mere mention of Param Krupalu Dev.

› Background

Smt. Devba was the daughter of Shri Raghavjibhai of Maliya. She was born into a Jain family and was deeply rooted in the Jain faith from childhood. Her marriage was to Shri Ravjibhai, who belonged to a Vaishnav family in Vavania. Despite the difference in religious traditions between her natal and marital families, Devba continued to observe the Jain way of life after marriage — a testament to the depth of her spiritual convictions and the tolerant atmosphere of the household.

The family had settled in Vavania after Param Krupalu Dev's paternal grandfather, Shri Panchanbhai, moved there from Manekwada in V.S. 1892 (1836 A.D.) and established a shipping and moneylending business.

› Birth of Param Krupalu Dev

A yogini named Rambaiba, who resided in Vavania, predicted that Devba would bear an extraordinary son. This prophecy was fulfilled when Shrimad Rajchandra was born as the second of her six children on Kartik Purnima, V.S. 1924 (9 November 1867 A.D.), in Vavania.

From the earliest years, it was evident that the child was no ordinary soul. The remarkable spiritual capacities that Param Krupalu Dev displayed — including the recollection of past lives at the age of seven — would have been witnessed by Devba first-hand.

› Nature and character

The book describes Smt. Devba as simple, cultured, and an epitome of good character, loving kindness and humility. She was extraordinarily soft-hearted — her eyes would well up at the mere mention of her son Param Krupalu Dev. This tenderness of heart reflected her deep maternal bond as well as her recognition of the spiritual greatness of her child.

› Children

Devba and Ravjibhai had six children:

  1. Shivkunvarben — the eldest, married Shri Chatrabhuj Bechar of Jetpar
  2. Shrimad Rajchandra — the second child and subject of the book
  3. Meenaben — married Shri Tokarshi Pitamber from Anjar, Kutch
  4. Jhabakben — married Shri Jasraj Doshi of Vavania
  5. Shri Mansukhbhai — joined the business firm after V.S. 1951; Param Krupalu Dev willed His assets to him; co-published the first edition of 'Shrimad Rajchandra' with Ambalalbhai
  6. Jijiben — the youngest, married Shri Jhaverchand Malukchand from Sayla

› Param Krupalu Dev's renunciation

When the question of Param Krupalu Dev's formal renunciation arose, Shri Devkaranji Muni — who was initiated as a monk alongside Lalluji Muni — personally spoke to Devba to request her permission for Param Krupalu Dev to take up the monastic path. This incident reflects the importance that was given to Devba's consent and the respect with which she was regarded in matters concerning her son's spiritual journey.
