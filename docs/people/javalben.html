Smt. Javalben was Param Krupalu Dev's first daughter and second child overall, born on Kartik Sud Pancham (Jnan Pancham) in V.S. 1948 (1892 AD). She passed away in V.S. 2034.

Javalben and her husband Shri Bhagvanbhai Modi (son of Ranchodbhai of Dharampur) bought land in Vavaniya and constructed a temple there in 1946 AD, 45 years after Param Krupalu Dev's dehvilay.
