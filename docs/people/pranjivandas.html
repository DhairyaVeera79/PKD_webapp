Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta

Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta
Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta
Relation Param Krupalu Dev's wife's uncle (father-in-law's brother)
Profession Chief Medical Officer of Idar State
Brothers Shri Popatlalbhai Mehta (eldest), Shri Revashankarbhai Jagjivan
Significance Introduced Param Krupalu Dev and Gandhiji; hosted Param Krupalu Dev during Idar retreat

Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta was Param Krupalu Dev's wife's uncle — the youngest of the Jagjivan brothers of Morbi, alongside Shri Popatlalbhai Mehta (Param Krupalu Dev's father-in-law) and Shri Revashankarbhai Jagjivan (Param Krupalu Dev's business partner). He served as the Chief Medical Officer of Idar State. He played a pivotal role in Param Krupalu Dev's life by introducing Him to Mahatma Gandhiji — their historic first meeting in Jeth V.S. 1947 took place at Dr. Pranjivandas's home in Mumbai. He also sponsored Gandhiji's journey to London to become a barrister. When Gandhiji returned to India, he stayed at Dr. Pranjivandas's home, and it was here that the fateful meeting with Param Krupalu Dev took place.

Dr. Pranjivandas also hosted Param Krupalu Dev at his residence in Idar during Param Krupalu Dev's retreat there in V.S. 1955. Based on his insistence, Param Krupalu Dev had first visited Idar in V.S. 1953. Param Krupalu Dev had noted that when Dr. Pranjivandas went abroad for his medical studies, his mannerisms, food habits, and attitude changed — it took time for him to return to the Indian way. Once he did, Param Krupalu Dev praised: "Have Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta sadai par aavi gaya chhe" (Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta has crossed over to the right side).
