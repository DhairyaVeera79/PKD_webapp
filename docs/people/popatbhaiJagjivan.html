Shri Popatlalbhai Mehta

Shri Popatlalbhai Mehta
Shri Popatlalbhai Mehta
Relation Father-in-law
Native place Morbi
Brothers Shri Revashankarbhai Jagjivan, Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta
Daughter Smt. Jhabakben
Significance Eldest of the four Jagjivan brothers

Shri Popatlalbhai Mehta was the eldest of the four Jagjivan brothers of Morbi, and the father of Smt. Jhabakben — Param Krupalu Dev's wife. He was thus Param Krupalu Dev's father-in-law. His younger brothers were Shri Revashankarbhai Jagjivan and Dr. Pranjivandas Mehta.
