Shri Chatrabhuj Bechar
Shri Chatrabhuj Bechar
Native place Jetpar
Relation Brother-in-law (Shivkunvarben's husband)
Significance Param Krupalu Dev frequently stayed at his home in Jetpar

Shri Chatrabhuj Bechar of Jetpar was Param Krupalu Dev's brother-in-law — the husband of Smt. Shivkunvarben, Param Krupalu Dev's eldest sister. Param Krupalu Dev frequently stayed at his home in Jetpar.
