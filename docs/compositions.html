Major Compositions by Param Krupalu Dev

  • Shri Atmasiddhi Shastra (The Six Fundamentals) — Crown jewel. 142 verses in ~1.5 hours. Composed Aso Vad 1, V.S. 1952 (~October 1896) at Nadiad. Dialogue between Guru and Shishya establishing six fundamental truths. Ambalalbhai served as amanuensis.
  • Mokshmala (Garland of Liberation) — First published work. 108 essays on spiritual topics. Published V.S. 1944 in Ahmedabad. Written at ~age 16. Recommended to Gandhiji. Juthabhai helped with publication.
  • Bhavnabodh (Understanding of Reflections) — Prose treatise on twelve bhavnas (contemplations) essential for spiritual progress in the Jain tradition.
  • Apurva Avsar (The Unprecedented Occasion) — Poem expressing deep yearning for liberation and the unprecedented spiritual opportunity of the present birth. Composed during period of intense retreat.
  • Mool Marg Sambhlo Jinno Re — Devotional hymn on the fundamental path of the Jinas (enlightened beings).
  • Nirkhine Navyauvana — Poem on renunciation and dispassion; observing youth's transience. Regularly read by Gandhiji; influenced his vow of celibacy.
  • Samucchayvaycharya — Philosophical commentary/treatise written during Param Krupalu Dev's scholarly period.

Other Compositions & Writings

  • First poem (Vavania) — Earliest known poetic composition, written during childhood in Vavania.
  • Hey Prabhu Hey Prabhu — Devotional poem composed at Ralaj.
  • Yam Niyam — Composition on spiritual disciplines, composed at Ralaj.
  • Bina Nayan — Devotional composition.
  • Chhah Pad no Patra (6 Pad no Patra) — Letter in verse form containing six stanzas.
  • Aath Drashti ni Sajay — Composition on the eight perspectives/viewpoints.
  • Dhanya Re Divas Aa Aho — Devotional hymn.
  • Ichhe Chhe Je Yogijan (Rajkot) — Spiritual poem composed during the Rajkot period.
  • Sahajatmaswaroop Paramguru — Devotional invocation/mantra.
  • Autobiography (Atmakatha) — Brief autobiographical sketch written by Param Krupalu Dev Himself.
  • Poems during retreats — Various devotional and philosophical verses composed during Kavitha, Uttarsanda, and Idar retreats.

Letters (Patravyavahar)

~920 letters total, forming a major corpus of Param Krupalu Dev's philosophical and spiritual instruction.

  • Letters to Saubhagyabhai — ~244 letters. Largest collection. Deeply personal and philosophical.
  • Letters to Ambalalbhai — ~127 letters. Instructional and philosophical guidance.
  • Letters to Lalluji Muni — 92 letters. Guidance on monastic practice and inner awakening.
  • Letters to Juthabhai — ~20 letters. To his first devotee.
  • Letters to Gandhiji — 3 surviving letters (bundle lost in a London bus). Included 27 questions and answers.

Posthumous Compilations

  • Compilation 'Shrimad Rajchandra' — First edition V.S. 1961, compiled by Ambalalbhai and Mansukhbhai. Contains ~920 letters, poems, prose, philosophical writings. Gandhiji wrote preface for 2nd edition.
  • Updeshchhaya (Shadow of Instruction) — Collection of Param Krupalu Dev's verbal instructions recorded by devotees.
  • Updeshnondh — Notes of Param Krupalu Dev's instructions and discourses.
  • Vyakhyansaar — Summaries of Param Krupalu Dev's lectures and discourses.

Books about Shrimad Rajchandra

  • 'Shrimad Rajchandra ni Jeevanrekha' — By Shri Mansukhbhai Kiratchand Mehta. Biography/life sketch.
  • 'Sakshat Saraswati' — By Shri Vanechand Popatbhai Daftari. Records of avdhan demonstrations.
  • 'Shrimad Rajchandra Jeevankala' — By Brahmachariji Shri Govardhandasji. Life and spiritual attainments.
  • 'Shrimad Rajchandra - Jeevanjyoti' — By Munishri Punyavijayji (Jignasu). Life history.
  • 'Adhyatma Rajchandra' — By Dr. Bhagwandas Mehta. Spiritual analysis.
  • 'Shrimad Rajchandra Jeevan-Sadhana' — By Mukulbhai Kalarthi. Life and spiritual practice.
  • 'Shrimad Rajchandra ane Gandhiji' — Edited by Mukulbhai Kalarthi. Relationship between Param Krupalu Dev and Gandhi.
  • 'Shrimad Rajchandra – Jeevan ane Kavan' — By Pujya Gurudevshri Rakeshji. Gujarati original (2001); English translation 'Saga of Spirituality' by Sharnarpit Renu Jhaveri (2014).

Scriptural & Classical Works Referenced

  • 'Bhadrabahusanhita' — By Chaud Purvadhar Shri Bhadrabahuswami. Astrology text; Param Krupalu Dev mastered it rapidly at Anupchandbhai's home in Bharuch.
  • 'Yogavashishtha' — Hindu philosophical text. Recommended by Param Krupalu Dev to Gandhiji.
  • 'Panchikaran' — Recommended by Param Krupalu Dev to Gandhiji.
  • 'Maniratnamala' — Recommended by Param Krupalu Dev to Gandhiji.
  • 'Shaddarshansamucchay' — Treatise on six philosophies. Recommended by Param Krupalu Dev to Gandhiji.
  • 'Samaysaarnatak' — By Pandit Shri Banarasidas. Four verses quoted by Param Krupalu Dev to Saubhagyabhai.
  • 'Shri Anandghan Chovisi' — By Avdhoot Shri Anandghanji Maharaj. Devotional compositions chanted by Param Krupalu Dev during retreats.
  • 'Uttaradhyayan Sutra' — Jain canonical text referenced in teachings.
