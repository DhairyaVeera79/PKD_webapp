Mokshmala city skyline
Mokshmala
Country India
State Gujarat
Population 194,947 (2011)
Known for Ceramics industry, Shrimad Rajchandra

Param Krupalu Dev composed Mokshmala at age 16 years 5 months in 3 days at house of Vanechand Popatbhai Daftary. By this age he had read all the sutras of Vedanta and Jainism adnd then decided to follow Jain way of life as he resonated with that more. 108 chapters. There are stories from Jin Agams (most from Utaradayan sutra) we at age 16 feel these are heavy topics, but imagine Param Krupalu Dev wrote this at age 16; Dos/Donts; philosophical talks. 3 sections: Samyag darshan, Samyag Jnan, Samyag Charitra. And the overall length of chapters is very concise. Param Krupalu Dev gives Mokshmala to read to chikar people. If someone asks him what to read, so Param Krupalu Dev tells first thing - Mokshmala. Bappa says kid's first education should be Mokshmala. After some time, in Vavania , in Upashray, Sadhvijis used to learn Morkshmala from him like paathshala. In starting, sadhvi jis used to sit on table and Param Krupalu Dev on floor (that tile also has been preserved and kept on which Param Krupalu Dev used to sit and teach), but after some time the sadhvijis realised that Param Krupalu Dev is a Mahatma purush, but Param Krupalu Dev never sat on the table so sadhvijis then would come sit down.

› Composition of Mokshmala

The peacock emblem of Morbi, representing the city's name meaning "abode of peacocks".

Composed by Param Krupalu Dev at age 16 years 5 months in 3 days at house of Vinaychandrabhai Popatbhai Daftary in Chaitra month of 1940 V.S (1884 AD). 108 chapters. There are stories from Jin Agams (most from Uttaradhyayana sutra); Dos/Donts; philosophical talks. 3 sections: Samyag darshan, Samyag Jnan, Samyag Charitra. And the overall length of chapters is very concise.

› Printing of Mokshmala at Ahmedabad

At age 20, 1944 V.S (1888 AD) Param Krupalu Dev did not have money to publish Mokshmala. He had to collect money. Veraval's Nancybhai sponsored him some money. He goes to Ahmedabad as printing over there is cheaper than bombay. But Param Krupalu Dev did not know anyone in Ahmedabad. Vanechand Popatbhai Daftary said that he knows a person who is renowned and well established and will help you, in this way wrote a letter. The letter was written addressing Jaisinghbhai Ujamsingh, elder brother of Pujyashri Juthabhai which Param Krupalu Dev took and went to Ahmedabad, and which's content wrote that the person bringing in this letter (Param Krupalu Dev) is a really scholar person, is a Shatavdhani (and Shatavdhani that time had a seperate fan base) and please help him in any way you can. And now if a 20 year old kid comes to you, what happens? So he tells Param Krupalu Dev that he is very busy in his business and does not have time and tell his yonger brother Pujyashri Juthabhai to help him any way he can.
